OUR STORY

A Taste of Vietnam

NNB Dessert House — Nhung Nguoi Ban, meaning "Friends" — celebrates Vietnamese late-night dessert culture. From hot and cold chè soups with toppings chosen from a glass cabinet, to homemade jellies, flan and street snacks, every dish is made the way friends and family in Vietnam have always made it.

Hidden down a fairy-lit alleyway off Hampshire Road, our Sunshine shop is the perfect place for a summer evening craving — sweet or savoury.

  • Authentic Vietnamese desserts (Chè, Yogurt, Crème Caramel)
  • Traditional family recipes
  • Vegetarian & vegan options available
  • Dine-in, takeaway & delivery

ABOUT US

More Than Just Dessert

NNB Dessert House (Nhung Nguoi Ban — "Friends") is a Vietnamese dessert and street-food spot hidden down a fairy-lit alleyway in Sunshine, with a second location in Footscray. Our menu celebrates Vietnamese late-night food culture: an array of chè with toppings chosen from a glass cabinet, homemade yogurts and jellies, plus savoury snacks like bột chiên, bò né, bún riêu and rice paper salad.

It's the perfect place for a summer evening craving — sweet or savoury — over coconut milk chè, crushed ice and flan.
